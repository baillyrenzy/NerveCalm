Daily Wellness

Small habits that work with NerveCalm to keep you feeling steady.

A calm routine is built, not bought

A capsule can support your routine, but it works best beside good habits. These simple, practical ideas cost almost nothing and fit into a normal day.

Sleep rhythm

Go to bed and wake at the same times, even on weekends. Keep the room cool, dark and quiet. Stop caffeine at least eight hours before bed.

Breathing breaks

Breathe in for four counts, hold for four, out for six. Repeat five times whenever you notice your shoulders rising.

Move every day

A brisk 20 to 30 minute walk is one of the easiest ways to clear your head and ease physical tension.

Eat steadily

Regular meals with protein, fiber and healthy fats help avoid the energy swings that make days feel harder.

Hydrate

Even mild dehydration can leave you tired and irritable. Keep a bottle of water within reach.

Screen limits

Give yourself a screen-free hour before bed. Read, stretch, journal or listen to quiet music instead.
